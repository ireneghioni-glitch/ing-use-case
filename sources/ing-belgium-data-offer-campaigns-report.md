### 2.1 Transition Timeline & Account Migration Rules
* **Effective Launch Date**: May 21, 2026 [160, 164, 235, 483, 487, 531, 534].
* **2-Month Price Protection Trial**: Customers received automatic access to new pack features while retaining legacy account pricing from May 21, 2026, until **August 1, 2026** [160, 161, 164, 236, 483, 484, 487, 530, 531, 534].
* **Automatic Conversion Mapping**:
  * *Lion Account / Do Basic* $\rightarrow$ **ING Go Pack** [168, 236, 490, 537].
  * *ING Green Account / Do More* $\rightarrow$ **ING More Pack** [168, 236, 490, 537].
* **Account Continuity**: IBANs, debit/credit card plastic, PIN codes, standing orders, and direct debits remained 100% unchanged during migration [170, 236, 493, 539].

### 4.1 Minor Accounts Portfolio Breakdown

#### 1. ING Go to 18 Current Account (Ages 8 to 17)
* **Pricing**: 100% Free (no monthly account or debit card management fees) [8, 35, 406, 410, 420].
* **Legal Framework & Opening**:
  * Belgian law does not set a statutory age limit, relying on the doctrine of cognitive capacity and discernment [3, 396].
  * ING sets **age 8** as the operational threshold for account creation [3, 397, 412].
  * Minor cannot open independently; application requires authorization and digital signature by legal representative (parent/guardian) using itsme® or card reader [4, 8, 381, 397, 412, 420].
* **Account Mechanics**:
  * Minor is the sole legal account holder [6, 413].
  * Strict ban on overdrafts (positive balance operation only) [6, 398].
  * Includes 1 debit card (Bancontact/Maestro) for free SEPA cash withdrawals and worldwide payments [35, 407, 411, 420]. Free first lost/stolen replacement (€10 thereafter) [409].
  * Wallet integrations: **Apple Pay** available from age 13; **Google Pay** available from age 16 [46, 408]. Includes Wero-powered P2P split bill requests [408].
  * Automatic conversion into an **ING Go Pack** on 18th birthday (maintained free until age 26) [9, 415, 421].

#### 2. Parental Governance via "Children Overview" App Interface
Parents manage minor accounts through a dedicated "Children Overview" space inside their master ING Banking App (available for kids from age 10) [44, 46, 393, 414]:
* **Real-Time Limits Control**: Customize daily/weekly payment caps and cash withdrawal limits [36, 48, 394, 409, 414].
* **1-Click Card Freeze**: Temporarily pause/unpause card spending instantly without calling Card Stop [45, 394, 414].
* **Transaction Transparency & Push Alerts**: Real-time spending notifications and categorized transaction tracking [38, 45, 48, 51, 394].
* **Allowance Automation**: Set up recurring scheduled allowance transfers from parent profile [38, 45, 394, 407, 414].
* **Shared Management (Co-Parent Access)**: Add a secondary legal guardian by submitting official legal proof (household composition, birth certificate, court ruling) verified by back-office teams [49, 50, 394].

#### 3. ING Save Up for Minors (Ages 10 to 17)
* Programmatic micro-savings service rounding up daily debit card transactions to the next euro, transferring cumulative rounded spare change to a linked savings account daily [215, 216, 395].
* **Savings Accelerator (x3)**: Option to triple saved amounts (e.g., €2.40 purchase $\rightarrow$ €0.60 standard round-up $\rightarrow$ **€1.80 saved**) [217, 218].

## 6. Synthesis of Strategic Conclusions for Competitive Intelligence

1. **Packaging Strategy vs. Unbundled Fees**: ING Belgium's shift to bundled subscriptions creates high customer retention by embedding non-banking perks (Amazon Prime worth €2.99/mo, AXA/Chubb insurance suites) [9, 15, 61, 68, 155, 158, 236, 391, 478, 482, 525, 529, 573]. Competitors relying on unbundled fees face positioning challenges against ING's €1.90/month youth rate [390, 391].
2. **Early Lifecycle Lock-In**: The "Go to 18" free current account combined with the "Children Overview" parental controls locks in family banking relationships early, providing seamless automatic conversion to adult packs at age 18 [9, 44, 46, 393, 415, 421].
3. **Data-Driven Campaign Execution**: Promotional strategy relies on direct cash matches (€50 welcome bonuses) and retail brand vouchers (HEMA) tied to strict calendar windows (Back-to-School, Spring/Summer peaks) to maximize activation velocity [59, 144, 381, 384, 419, 473, 587].
