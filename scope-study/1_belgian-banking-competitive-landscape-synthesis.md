## 1. Map of Recurring Themes, Messages, and Values

Competitors in the Belgian market structure their customer communications around five primary strategic pillars:

### 1.1 Super-Apps & "Beyond Banking" Ecosystems vs. Focused Core Banking
*   **The Ecosystem Approach (KBC & Belfius)**: Position mobile banking apps as daily lifestyle platforms. KBC integrates public transport ticketing (NMBS, De Lijn), parking payments, merchant cashbacks (*Kate Deals*), multi-currency sub-accounts, and international mobile data passes (*Firsty eSIM*). Belfius embeds carbon tracking via Doconomy (*Banx*) and e-commerce payment integrations.
*   **The Core Banking Focus (ING Belgium)**: Positioned by leadership (CEO Peter Adams) around radical simplicity and deep financial expertise, deliberately choosing to pass on broad non-banking lifestyle ecosystems (such as sports scores or telecom resale) to maintain app usability and product transparency.

### 1.2 Sustainability, ESG, and "Slow Banking"
*   **"Slow Banking" & Carbon Tracking**: Belfius's digital venture *Banx* promotes sustainable banking through carbon footprint tracking on purchases (Doconomy) and NGO partnerships (Natuurpunt/Natagora).
*   **FairFin & Ethical Indexing**: Argenta leverages its 75% score on the *BankWijzer* sustainability index as a core competitive argument against larger incumbents.
*   **Regulatory & Social Alignment**: ESG is increasingly treated as a baseline market requirement ("qualifier, not a winner"), with banks aligning mortgages and commercial loans to EPC energy ratings and European Taxonomy standards.

### 1.3 Youth and Gen Z Acquisition Funnels
*   **Gamified & Influencer-Led Onboarding**: Competitors target the under-25 demographic through native social media storytelling (Revolut's TikTok campaigns with Rhodé Kok), student pop-up tours (KBC's 18-campus camper van roadshow), and gamified app challenges (*Level Up Your Mobile* with Samir Korbi).
*   **Societal & Purpose Movements**: Belfius mobilizes youth engagement via the *JEZ!* charity movement across DPG Media networks (VTM, Qmusic, HLN).
*   **Early Lock-In & Transition Pipelines**: ING Belgium deploys the *ING Go to 18* account (ages 8–17) with the *Children Overview* parental control suite, paired with micro-savings (*ING Save Up for Minors*), establishing automatic conversion into adult packs at age 18.

### 1.4 Conversational AI and Hyper-Personalization
*   **Cognitive Banking (KBC)**: Deploys the *Kate AI* assistant (3.7M users, 30M conversations, 60% autonomous resolution rate in Belgium) integrated with *Personetics Engage* to deliver proactive, real-time financial insights (47.4M cumulative insights delivered).
*   **AE Personalization Maturity Model**: Insurers and banks evaluate progression from static mass campaigns across 6 maturity levels toward 1:1 real-time predictive conversations.

### 1.5 B2B Mobility Budget Transition (SME Electrification)
*   Driven by Belgian federal legislation mandating a formal Mobility Budget for companies with 50 or more employees starting January 1, 2027, B2B messaging heavily focuses on flexible car leasing (*LIZY*, *Ayvens*, *Belfius Auto Lease*) and electric fleet management.

## 2. Strategic Communication Gaps & Differentiators: ING Belgium vs. Competitors

│ • Streamlined 4-Pack Model    │ • Lacks Broad Non-Banking Ecosystems   │
│ • Unrestricted Wallet Access  │ • No Conversational AI Engine (vs Kate) │

│ • Strong Public Trust Rating  │ • Higher Account Restructuring Fees     │

### 2.1 Where ING Belgium Differentiates (Competitive Strengths)
1.  **Transparent 4-Tier Subscription Architecture**: Effective May 21, 2026, ING consolidated legacy accounts into 4 clear packs (*ING Go, More, Extra, Max*). *ING More* (€7.90/mo standard or €3.90/mo with deposits >= €700) bundles Amazon Prime BE and multi-party insurance coverage via the *Protection Hub*.
2.  **Unrestricted Mobile Wallet Policy**: ING provides Apple Pay and Google Pay across **all account tiers** (including the entry-level *ING Go*), whereas KBC strategically excludes Apple Pay from its entry-level *Basic Account* (€2.00/mo) to force upgrades to the *Plus Account* (€3.75/mo).
3.  **Embedded Multi-Party Insurance Protections**: Through its *Protection Hub*, ING directly embeds travel, cyber, device, home assistance, and purchase coverage (underwritten by AXA, Chubb, Europ Assistance, Allianz, and Collinson) into its standard packages.
4.  **Superior Independent Trust Score**: ING maintains a **3.8/5 Trustpilot rating** (~1.9K reviews), significantly outperforming KBC (1.6/5), Argenta (1.7/5), and Belfius (1.3–1.5/5), which suffer from severe customer backlash over branch closures and rigid appointment systems.

### 2.2 Where ING Belgium Lags (Vulnerabilities & Competitor Advantages)
1.  **Absence of Native Super-App Services**: KBC and Belfius offer native transit ticket purchasing, parking, and multi-currency sub-accounts directly in-app, whereas ING deliberately passes on non-banking ecosystems.
2.  **Lack of a Conversational AI Engine**: KBC's *Kate* handles 30M conversations with a 60% resolution rate and delivers 6 personalized Personetics insights per user monthly. ING relies on the static *Protection Hub* interface.
3.  **Social-First Vertical Video Execution**: Revolut (*For You Agency*) and Belfius dominate TikTok performance marketing with cinematic vertical storytelling and Spark Ads, whereas ING relies more on traditional ATL agency sponsorships (TBWA\Belgium, Red Flames football partnership).
4.  **Digital Payment Restrictions at Entry-Level (Argenta Gap)**: Argenta offers a 100% free account (*Argenta Green*) but lacks native Apple Pay and Google Pay (requiring third-party Curve workarounds).
