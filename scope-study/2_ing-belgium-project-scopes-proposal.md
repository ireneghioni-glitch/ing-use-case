# Step 2: Strategic Project Scopes Proposal for ING Belgium: Competitive Intelligence Framework

## Executive Overview
Based on the comprehensive synthesis of the Belgian retail banking competitive landscape (*`belgian-banking-competitive-landscape-synthesis.md`*), this document defines **8 potential project scopes** for ING Belgium. These scopes span Strategic (bank-wide positioning), Tactical (product/campaign level), and Operational (channel/format level) intelligence gathering. 

Each scope is designed to address core business challenges identified in the *Marketing SPY* framework: establishing a reproducible, structured methodology to compare competitor campaigns, identifying communication style patterns, measuring outcomes, and ensuring strict regulatory compliance.

---

## Part 1: Detailed Breakdown of the 8 Project Scopes

### Scope 1: Youth & Gen Z Acquisition Funnel Benchmark
* **Scope Name:** Under-25 Acquisition Funnel & Campaign Benchmark: ING Belgium vs. KBC, Revolut, and Belfius
* **Level:** Tactical (Product / Campaign)
* **Description:** A comparative analysis of youth acquisition funnels and campaign messaging targeting the under-25 demographic (teens, students, and young workers). The scope evaluates onboarding friction, cash acquisition incentives (€50 cash match for ING vs. €20 Revolut cashback promo vs. KBC/Belfius free tiers), parental control suites (ING *Children Overview* vs. Revolut *<18*), experiential pop-up tours (KBC's 18-campus camper van roadshow), and youth movements (Belfius *JEZ!* charity campaign across DPG Media networks).
* **Competitors Involved:** 3 competitors (KBC Group, Revolut Belgium, Belfius Bank / Banx).
* **Channels:** TikTok, Instagram, Meta Ads Manager, Experiential Campus Roadshows, App Store landing pages.
* **Timeframe:** 2024 – 2026.
* **Feasibility with Current Sources:** **High**. Extensive primary data is available in current sources, including exact package pricing (€0/mo Go to 18; €1.90/mo More 18-25), promotion terms (€50 cash match, HEMA vouchers), creator campaign details (Rhodé Kok for Revolut), and TikTok ad benchmarks (CPM €3.00–€7.00, 2.95% TopView CTR).
* **Reproducibility:** Highly reproducible across future age cohorts (e.g., 26–35 young professionals) or other Benelux markets by replicating the audit criteria across ad libraries and app onboarding flows.
* **Compliance:** Requires strict adherence to FSMA guidelines on marketing financial products to minors, GDPR age threshold validation (Apple Pay at age 13, Google Pay at age 16), and mandatory display of statutory warnings (*"Geld lenen kost ook geld"*).
* **Impact for ING:** Directly optimizes conversion copy for ING's *Go to 18* and *Go/More 18–25* packs, strengthening early family lock-in and closing the creative gap against Revolut’s viral social campaigns.

### Scope 2: Daily Banking Subscription Architecture & Bundled Perks Audit
* **Scope Name:** Retail Account Packaging & Value-Added Bundling Audit: ING 4-Pack Model vs. Belgian Incumbents & Neobanks
* **Level:** Strategic (Bank-Wide)
* **Description:** A deep-dive strategic audit of daily banking subscription models, fee indexation trends, and value-added perk bundling. Evaluates ING Belgium’s 4-tier pack overhaul (*Go, More, Extra, Max* introduced May 21, 2026) against KBC (*Basic* vs. *Plus* with Apple Pay gating), Argenta (*Green, Silver, Gold*), Belfius (*Beats* tiers), and Revolut (*Standard* to *Ultra*). Focuses on pricing power, primary bank account deposit thresholds (€700/mo deposit for ING More discount), and embedded protections (*Protection Hub*: AXA, Chubb, Europ Assistance, Amazon Prime BE).
* **Competitors Involved:** 4 competitors (KBC, Argenta, Belfius, Revolut).
* **Channels:** Owned Web Portals, App Landing Pages, Press Releases, Independent Review Aggregators (Trustpilot, Test-Aankoop, Reddit).
* **Timeframe:** May 2024 – September 2026 (capturing ING's 4-pack launch and 2-month price protection trial).
* **Feasibility with Current Sources:** **High**. Complete product matrices, fee schedules, exact insurance coverage limits, and transition rules are fully documented in current sources.
* **Reproducibility:** Excellent. Can be re-executed annually following bank fee indexations or product tier updates across the Belgian market.
* **Compliance:** High necessity for transparent pricing disclosures under the EU Distance Marketing of Financial Services Directive (DMFSD) and PSD2 access guidelines; avoids unfair competitive comparison claims.
* **Impact for ING:** Validates ING More's value proposition (€19/mo standalone value priced at €7.90 or €3.90 discounted), equipping marketing teams with data-backed counter-messaging against free basic accounts (Argenta Green) or gated models (KBC Basic).

### Scope 3: Mobile Wallet Accessibility & Feature Gating Audit
* **Scope Name:** Digital Payment & Mobile Wallet Restrictions Audit: ING vs. KBC and Argenta
* **Level:** Operational (Channel / Format)
* **Description:** A targeted operational audit assessing mobile wallet availability (Apple Pay and Google Pay) and feature gating across entry-level vs. premium account tiers. Analyzes ING Belgium's policy of offering unrestricted Apple Pay and Google Pay across **all tiers** (including entry-level *ING Go* at €2.20/mo) versus KBC's strategic decision to exclude Apple Pay from *KBC Basic* (€2.00/mo) to force upgrades to *KBC Plus* (€3.75/mo), and Argenta’s total absence of native mobile wallets (requiring third-party Curve workarounds).
* **Competitors Involved:** 2 competitors (KBC Group, Argenta).
* **Channels:** Reddit (r/Belgium), Trustpilot, Mobile App Stores, Digital Product Feature Tables.
* **Timeframe:** 2024 – 2026.
* **Feasibility with Current Sources:** **High**. Specific tier gating rules, monthly fee differences, and user social media sentiment are documented in the notebook.
* **Reproducibility:** Replicable for assessing new digital payment methods (e.g., Wero P2P integration, Central Bank Digital Currency / Digital Euro readiness).
* **Compliance:** Low regulatory risk; factual product capability comparison compliant with Belgian fair competition standards.
* **Impact for ING:** Provides immediate, high-intent advertising acquisition hooks targeting dissatisfied KBC Basic and Argenta Green users seeking free, native Apple Pay access.

### Scope 4: Conversational AI & Cognitive Customer Engagement Audit
* **Scope Name:** AI Assistant & Hyper-Personalization Ecosystems: KBC Kate AI vs. ING Core Banking Positioning
* **Level:** Strategic (Bank-Wide)
* **Description:** Evaluates the strategic gap between KBC's cognitive banking model (*Kate AI*: 3.7M users, 30M conversations, 60% autonomous resolution, *Personetics Engage*: 47.4M delivered insights) and ING Belgium's deliberate focus on product simplicity and core banking expertise (CEO Peter Adams passing on broad non-banking super-app features). Analyzes customer adoption, engagement rates (35% for Personetics), user rating scores (4.65/5 for KBC insights), and the contrast with ING's static *Protection Hub*.
* **Competitors Involved:** 1 primary competitor (KBC Group).
* **Channels:** Mobile Banking Apps, Corporate IR Presentations, FinTech Industry Reports (Personetics case studies).
* **Timeframe:** 2023 – 2026.
* **Feasibility with Current Sources:** **High**. Detailed metrics on Kate AI performance, Personetics insight volumes, and executive strategic statements from both ING and KBC CEOs are present in the sources.
* **Reproducibility:** Highly scalable for assessing conversational AI maturity across other European banking markets (e.g., ING Netherlands, ING Germany).
* **Compliance:** High regulatory considerations regarding the EU AI Act, automated profiling under GDPR, and ethical AI transparency in financial advisory.
* **Impact for ING:** Informs C-level strategic decisions on whether ING Belgium should develop/acquire a conversational AI engine or double down on its "radical simplicity, transparency, and human trust" positioning.

### Scope 5: TikTok Paid Performance & Short-Form Video Execution Analysis
* **Scope Name:** Short-Form Vertical Video Performance Strategy: Revolut & Belfius vs. ING Creative Models
* **Level:** Operational (Channel / Format)
* **Description:** A tactical media and creative audit comparing short-form vertical video strategies on TikTok and Instagram. Analyzes Revolut's cinematic creator execution (*For You Agency* starring Rhodé Kok) and telemetry stack (AppsFlyer, SKAdNetwork, TikTok Pixel) alongside Belfius/Banx Spark Ads vs. ING Belgium's traditional agency sponsorship campaigns (TBWA\Belgium, Red Flames football partnership). Establishes creative production rules ("5-from-1" shoot rule) and benchmarks CPMs (€3.00–€7.00) and CTRs (2.95% TopView).
* **Competitors Involved:** 2 competitors (Revolut Belgium, Belfius Bank).
* **Channels:** TikTok Ads Manager, Instagram Reels, Meta Ad Library, Agency Portfolios (For You Agency, Kill Your Darlings).
* **Timeframe:** 2025 – 2026.
* **Feasibility with Current Sources:** **High**. Detailed agency playbooks, creator campaign mechanics, telemetry tool listings, and quantitative benchmark metrics exist in current sources.
* **Reproducibility:** Fully repeatable across any social media channel, video format, or product launch cycle.
* **Compliance:** Must comply with TikTok advertising policies, mandatory bilingual asset creation (French/Dutch asset parity for Belgium), and transparent disclosure of sponsored creator content.
* **Impact for ING:** Modernizes ING Belgium's social media performance marketing playbook, introducing structured telemetry and rapid creative iteration to reduce Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC).

### Scope 6: Public Trust vs. App Store Rating Divergence & Reputation Analysis
* **Scope Name:** Customer Reputation & Offline Friction Audit: Incumbent Branch Closures vs. ING Trust Scores
* **Level:** Strategic (Bank-Wide)
* **Description:** Investigates the "App Store - Trustpilot Paradox" in Belgian retail banking: high app store ratings (4.5–4.7 stars for KBC and Belfius) contrasting with abysmal independent public TrustScores (Belfius 1.3–1.5/5, KBC 1.6/5, Argenta 1.7/5) driven by physical branch closures, rigid appointment booking, and call center bottlenecks. Contrasts this friction with ING Belgium’s leading **3.8/5 Trustpilot rating** (~1.9K reviews).
* **Competitors Involved:** 3 competitors (Belfius Bank, KBC Group, Argenta).
* **Channels:** Trustpilot, Apple App Store, Google Play Store, Customer Service Portals, Social Listening Feeds (Engagor).
* **Timeframe:** 2024 – 2026.
* **Feasibility with Current Sources:** **High**. Specific star ratings, review counts, and detailed customer complaint categories (branch closures, appointment obstacles, phone redirections) are fully documented.
* **Reproducibility:** High. Can be applied to benchmark customer sentiment across any service industry undergoing rapid physical-to-digital channel migration.
* **Compliance:** Public sentiment analytics; requires objective sampling and data aggregation to avoid defamation or biased reporting.
* **Impact for ING:** Identifies specific competitor service vulnerabilities (e.g., account closure barriers, unhelpful call centers) to position ING's human-assisted digital support as a reliable, customer-centric alternative.

## Part 2: Evaluation & Scoring Matrix

To determine the most viable scope for a Proof of Concept (POC), each of the 8 scopes has been evaluated against 7 standardized criteria on a scale of **1 to 5** (where 1 = Very Low / Poor, 5 = Excellent / Very High):

1. **Strategic Relevance:** Alignment with ING Belgium's executive priorities and market positioning.
2. **Competitive Differentiation:** Ability to highlight clear gaps and unique advantages vs. competitors.
3. **Data Availability:** Density and completeness of ground-truth evidence in current notebook sources.
4. **Measurability:** Presence of concrete, quantifiable metrics (CPMs, rates, user volumes, ratings).
5. **Feasibility in 4–6 Weeks:** Ability to execute and deliver a complete POC within a 4 to 6-week window.
6. **Compliance & Bias Control:** Ease of maintaining regulatory compliance (FSMA, GDPR) and unbiased methodology.
7. **Scalability & Reproducibility:** Ease of replicating the analysis across other products, channels, or markets.

### Detailed Justification of Choice

#### 1. First Choice: Scope 1 — Youth & Gen Z Acquisition Funnel Benchmark
* **Why it was selected:** Capturing the under-25 demographic is the single highest-priority growth driver in Belgian retail banking, as youth acquisition establishes long-term customer lifetime value (LTV). Current notebook sources contain exceptionally rich, granular data across all major players: ING's *Go to 18* (€0/mo) and *More 18–25* (€1.90/mo vs €7.90 standard), KBC's *Young Person's Account* and camper van roadshows, Revolut's *<18* account and Rhodé Kok creator campaigns, and Belfius's *JEZ!* movement.
* **POC Value:** The POC will deliver a complete conversion funnel map, benchmarking onboarding steps, acquisition incentives (€50 ING cash match vs. €20 Revolut cashback), and creative hooks. It provides immediate, actionable recommendations to refine ING's Back-to-School and sports sponsorship copy.

#### 2. Second Choice: Scope 2 — Daily Banking Subscription Architecture & Perks Audit
* **Why it was selected:** ING Belgium executed a fundamental restructuring of its retail offering on May 21, 2026, introducing 4 consolidated packs. Current sources contain 100% complete data on ING's packs, exact insurance terms (*Protection Hub*), price protection trial rules, and full competitor fee structures (KBC Basic/Plus, Argenta Green/Silver/Gold, Revolut tiers).
* **POC Value:** A 4-week POC will build a comprehensive, objective pricing and value matrix. It proves the value proposition of *ING More* (€19/mo standalone value priced at €7.90 or €3.90 discounted with €700 deposit) and provides competitive intelligence to defend against competitor fee inflation claims while highlighting ING's included Amazon Prime BE and AXA/Chubb protections.

#### 3. Third Choice: Scope 3 — Mobile Wallet Accessibility & Feature Gating Audit
* **Why it was selected:** This scope focuses on a sharp, highly tangible competitive differentiator. KBC's strategy of gating Apple Pay behind its €3.75/mo Plus account and Argenta's complete lack of native mobile wallets create direct operational friction for consumers. ING offers unrestricted Apple Pay and Google Pay across all 4 packs (including the entry-level *Go* pack at €2.20/mo).
* **POC Value:** This POC can be executed in under 3 weeks with zero compliance risk. It delivers immediate direct-response marketing ad concepts (e.g., "Why pay €3.75/month just to use Apple Pay? Switch to ING Go for €2.20 with free Apple Pay included"), directly capitalizing on competitor product restrictions.
