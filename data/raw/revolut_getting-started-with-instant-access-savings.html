Getting started with Instant Access Savings

Revolut customers in Belgium can access individual Instant Access Savings. Instant Access Savings are available in the following currencies: EUR

Instant Access Savings are free to open and use. There are no fees, and you can withdraw at any time.

T&Cs apply.

Eligibility

Revolut customers in Belgium must meet all of the following criteria to open an Instant Access Savings account:

  • Must currently live in Belgium
  • Must be a Belgian tax resident, and provide the National Register Number
  • Must be a customer of the Belgian branch of Revolut Bank UAB

How to access Instant Access Savings

In the personal mobile app:

  • Go to 'Home' on the bottom menu
  • Below your balance, tap 'Accounts' and 'Add new'
  • Select 'Savings' and follow the prompts to set up your account

Or, simply use this link to create a Savings account.

Are there any minimum or maximum deposit amounts?

There's no minimum deposit amount. However, there's a maximum limit of 100,000 € on your Instant Access Savings deposits.

Your eligible deposits held with Revolut Bank UAB in aggregate are insured by the Lithuanian Deposit Insurance scheme. More information can be found in the Deposit Insurance Information document.
