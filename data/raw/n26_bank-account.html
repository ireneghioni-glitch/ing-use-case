N26 Smart

The bank account that puts you in the driver's seat

Want more control over your money? Our premium bank account comes with intuitive features that make it simple to budget, spend, and save. Manage your account from your smartphone, anywhere and anytime — for just €4.90 a month.

Your colorful N26 card — for at home or on the go

Choose from five different colors for your N26 Mastercard — Ocean, Rhubarb, Sand, Aqua, or Slate. Enjoy 3 free withdrawals in the eurozone per month, and make payments in foreign currencies without any extra fees. Plus, pay contactless or online with your virtual card, even before your physical card arrives.

Earning interest on your savings

No deposit limits**, straightforward conditions, and complete flexibility. Discover N26 Instant Savings, the non-regulated and accessible savings account available in the N26 app at no additional cost. 
More about the Instant Savings account

Save, spend, and budget with Spaces sub-accounts

With N26 Spaces, it's never been simpler to organize your money. Your Smart account comes with up to 10 Spaces — separate sub-accounts that you can use for saving or setting money aside. Create a Space, give it a name, and set your target amount — that’s it! Plus, the Rounds-Up feature lets you round-up card transactions to the nearest euro, then automatically puts the spare change into a Space of your choice.

Master your spending with Insights

Ready to get a bird's eye view of your finances? Enter N26 Insights, the feature that automatically categorizes your transactions as they happen. Instantly see if you've had a spike in spending on ‘Shopping,’ ‘Bars & Restaurants,’ ‘Groceries,’ and more — a quick look is all it takes for you to stay on top of your finances. Plus, with an easy overview of your scheduled regular payments, it’s simpler than ever to plan ahead and stay on track with your monthly budget.

Exclusive partner offers

Treat yourself to a few perks, courtesy of the N26 Smart premium bank account. Explore offers and deals from our partner brands, directly in your N26 app — they're exclusive to N26 customers and changing all the time.

Intuitive, beautifully simple banking

We think your bank's app should be as easy to use and nice to look at as any of your other favorite apps. That's why we've upgraded your banking experience, with modern design and streamlined money management tools — but without the hidden fees or piles of paperwork. And even better, it's all accessible from your smartphone or the desktop WebApp.
