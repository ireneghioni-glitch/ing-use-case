Card questions answered

Card types, limits, and what each personal plan includes.

The bunq Card lets you pay in stores and online, add cards to Apple Pay or Google Pay, and link each card to a Bank Account for clearer budgeting. Depending on your plan, you can create multiple Digital Cards and Physical Cards, check the app for your current limits.

Learn more

In general, a Credit Card is a payment card for shopping online or in-store (and sometimes withdrawing cash), often with a revolving line of credit.

At bunq, the Credit Card is funded with money you already hold, there’s no credit line or overdraft, while merchants still see a Mastercard Credit Card when that helps for holds or deposits.

Learn more

Getting a digital bunq Card is quick and easy, just a few taps in the bunq app. You can use it immediately with Apple Pay or Google Pay. Prefer a physical card? Order one through the app and have it delivered to your home.

Learn more

A Bank Card is a payment method that lets you shop in stores and online, and withdraw cash at ATMs. With bunq, you can choose Credit, Debit, Metal, or Digital Cards linked to your Bank Accounts, limits depend on your plan.

Learn more
