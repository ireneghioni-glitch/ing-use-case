Online bank account FAQs

  • To open a Revolut bank account, you need to:

    • download the Revolut app for iOS or Android
    • be at least 18 years old
    • be a legal resident of a supported country
    • have a valid government-issued document for identity verification
    • provide some personal details like your name, phone number, address, and a few others

    Don't worry, we'll walk you through everything in-app. Ready to get started?

    Download the app.

  • You can open and use a Revolut bank account on our Standard plan at no cost — with no set-up or maintenance fees. You can also upgrade to one of our paid plans for extra perks and discounts.

    Compare our plans.

  • You can have one Revolut bank account. With so many sub-accounts to help you organise your money, one is all you need. Check these out in-app to take your current account a step further.

    • Joint accounts: create a shared sub-account with another person to manage your money together.
    • Revolut Pro for freelancers: get a separate IBAN to run your business, plus add-on features like invoicing.
    • Revolut <18 for kids and teens: create sub-accounts for your kids, so they can learn to manage their money under your supervision.
    • Currency accounts: keep 38 in separate balances, with local account details that make it easy to send and receive money.
    • Pockets: create separate spaces to set aside money for different purposes and track your spending.

    It's like having multiple accounts, but better — because they're all in one place.

    Heads up — fees may apply for certain features. Check out the latest pricing in-app.

  • We use two-factor authentication to make sure you're the only one who can log into your account, and we'll send you anti-fraud warnings in-app if we suspect that a transfer may be a scam. We also protect your personal data with our full-stack security systems.

    Additionally, we use other security methods that vary depending on the Revolut entity you're registered to.
