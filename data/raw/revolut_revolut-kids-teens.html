Get started

How I can get Revolut?

All it takes is 3 simple steps.

Get started

  1. Download

    Download the Revolut — Kids & Teens app and open an account in minutes.

  2. Get approved

    Your parent will get a notification to approve your account from their own Revolut app. If they don’t have an account with us, they’ll need to create one.

  3. And go

    Get your Revolut card and personalise it your way.

    Then, create your virtual card and add it to your Apple or Google Wallet to start spending straight away.

    Age restrictions and personalisation fees apply.

Revolut — Kids & Teens accounts are available for individuals aged 6–15. A parent or legal guardian must be at least 18 years old. Under 13? Get your parents to download the main Revolut app and get you on-board. Payments to and from customers on Revolut — Kids & Teens are only available to teens aged 13+.
